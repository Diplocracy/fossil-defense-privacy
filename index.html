Privacy Policy for Fossil Defense

Fossil Defense respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how it is protected.

1. Data We Collect

Fossil Defense collects minimal data required for gameplay, analytics, and account functionality:

  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • Gameplay analytics (levels played, progress)
  • In-app purchase information
  • Player ID (via PlayFab)

2. How We Use the Data

  • To save game progress
  • To enable online features such as chat and friend interactions
  • To improve game performance and stability
  • To process purchases through Google Play

3. Third-Party Services

We use trusted services:

  • PlayFab – account and cloud saves
  • Google Play Billing – in-app purchases
  • Google Play Games – achievements
  • Unity – engine diagnostics

4. No Selling of Personal Data

Your data is never sold or shared for advertising purposes.

5. Children’s Privacy

Our game is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
